The series follows one research decision: understand what the shorts contribute,
locate their shared rebound risk, then evaluate ways to manage it.

## 1. Understanding Your P&L

**Question:** How do positions and common exposures explain portfolio earnings
and daily risk?

Start with the actual book P&L. Show sector contributions, explain the fitted
factor return, then multiply it by the exposure held. Keep the numerical sign
example and daily explorer. Put estimation details in an expandable section.
End with the losing short book's daily diversification and its reversal during
recoveries. Aim for roughly 1,500 words, four figures and one worked example.

## 2. Why the Short Book Struggles in Rebounds

**Question:** Do the shorts hold stocks that rebound faster than the longs?

Follow 2009 and 2020 through the decline and recovery. Show the higher beta,
volatility and prior losses of the shorts. Keep actual stock entries and exits,
then test recurrence across all 11 episodes at 21, 63 and 126 sessions. Explain
how book size connects relative stock gains to actual portfolio P&L. Aim for
roughly 1,800 words; each interactive display answers one question.

## 3. Managing Rebound Risk with Exposure Limits

**Question:** What did the tested controls improve, and what did they give up?

Define the volatility-rank limit and its rebalance timing. Compare both caps,
fast and slower portfolio scaling, and constant-size controls. Show net/gross
earnings, drawdown, turnover and the recovery trade-offs. End with the next
targeted daily-band test. Aim for roughly 1,200 words and two comparison tables.

The first post supplies the accounting; the second establishes the mechanism;
the third evaluates intervention. Each post links to the next question and
keeps the mathematics needed to interpret its own evidence.
