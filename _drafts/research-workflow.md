I want to be able to revisit a research idea and follow the reasoning: which paper motivated it, what I tested, and why I kept or rejected the change. I’m building a small setup to make those connections easier to maintain.

I keep papers, saved articles and notes in a local library. A search tool lets my AI assistant retrieve relevant passages, then read the surrounding pages. References retain the document title, version and page number, so I can check an argument against its original source.

Alongside that library, I’ve started an experiment registry. It records the question, the test, the evidence and the decision. Failed and inconclusive attempts stay visible. A completed run, an encouraging result and a decision to use it are recorded separately.

I want my blog posts to draw on those records: explain the question, show the comparison and make my judgment explicit. When an interpretation changes, keeping the earlier reasoning gives me something concrete to revisit.
